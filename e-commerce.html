01 About Project

Ubuntu living is a modern furniture e-commerce website designed to help customers browse, compare, and purchase furniture online with ease. The project focuses on creating a seamless shopping experience through intuitive navigation, clear product presentation, and a visually appealing interface that reflects the brand's premium yet locally inspored identity

02 Problem Statement

Many furniture webistes overwhelm uses with clustured layouts, confusing navigation, and insufficient product information. Customers often struggle to compare products, evaluate quality, and make confident purchasing decisions online. This can lead to fustrations, abondoned carts, and reducced trust in the brand.

03 Goals

Improve product discovery

Simplify furniture browsing

Incrase customer confindence

Create premium shopping experience
